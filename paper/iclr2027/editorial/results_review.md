# Results and final PDF review — 2026-09-10

- The primary quality comparison includes all 13 released Qwen3-8B tasks (1,243 examples), with seven methods in three metric tables: Perturbation, REAGENT, CLP, IFR, AttnLRP, FT, and DT. VT and HotpotQA recovery is excluded by the requested scope; their RISE and MAS rows remain. MATH and MoreHopQA have no released recovery metric.
- The combined CSV contains 91 method/task rows and 224 reported metric cells. All original DT/FT values are preserved. The 160 additional values are traced to 95 byte-verified published CSVs and checked against the publication. The IFR MQ-Q8 recursive aggregation exception and fast perturbation variants are recorded explicitly. Best observed task means for DT are 9/13 RISE, 11/13 MAS, and 6/6 reported recovery; no significance claim is made.
- RISE uses signed DT ranking; MAS and recovery use its positive part. Published full-task FT results and the separately executed development FT hop settings are distinguished. The 32 development observations are in the appendix.
- Both case figures retain their original exact tokens, signed scores, and DT curves. Each adds its own model-matched one-hop FT curve, making four original 21-point curves per figure. The y-axis states normalized full-response log-likelihood. Normalization is reconstructed from recorded full-response-plus-EOS scores with the released endpoint, clipping, and cumulative-minimum operations. DT and FT share identical endpoint scores within each model and example. Curve and color legends are in the right column below the deletion plot.
- Efficiency figures use existing paired warm measurements only: Qwen3 FT/DT attribution, Qwen3 dense/tiled implementation, and Qwen3.5 serial B1 versus real B2 with GPU checkpoints. Separate retention improvements are tabulated. Complete call boundaries, loading/warmup exclusions, implementation snapshots, and decimal GB including resident weights are stated. No new model evaluation or unmeasured length sweep was run.
- The final 17-page PDF was rendered by Poppler at 110 dpi. All pages were visually inspected; the full quality tables and case-figure page were additionally inspected individually. Main text ends on page 9; references are on pages 9–10 and the appendix begins on page 11. No clipped figures, overlapping legends, overfull boxes, missing references, or compiler warnings remain. Official template files are unchanged.
